How to Play

Cliffside Firefly Catching

  • Look & Listen: Native lyrics (Spanish or Chinese) are sung first, so their fireflies emerge from the sunset horizon first, followed by their English echoes. Use the Firefly Words toggle to practice Song Language only, English only, or Both.
  • Catch Fireflies: Click or tap any approaching word firefly in 3D space (or hold & sweep through them with a golden ink trail, or press 1–4 / Space). Caught words arc smoothly onto your A4 sketchbook right where the magical pen writes them.
  • No Game Over & Optional Decoys: Decoys are borrowed from other songs' lyrics and can be turned off (0) or scaled (Less → More) with the Decoys slider. Wrong catches or misses never end the song — missed words save to your Study Again deck, and catching them cleanly on a replay automatically graduates them to Mastered!
  • Live Controls & Shortcuts: Adjust Pace (4.0s), Speed (1.0×), Decoys, Firefly Words, Guidance, SFX & Volume right in the top bar while playing. Press Tab or → (or click Skip →) to skip instrumental breaks; P Play/Pause, R Study Deck, Esc Song Menu.
