How to Play

Cliffside Firefly Catching

  • Look & Listen: Native lyrics (e.g. Spanish) are sung first, so their fireflies emerge from the sunset horizon first, followed by their English echoes.
  • Catch Fireflies: Click or tap any approaching word firefly in 3D space (or hold & sweep through them, or press 1–4 / Space). Caught words swoop onto your A4 sketchbook as the magical pen writes them in ink.
  • No Game Over: Catching a decoy or being too late never loses the game. Missed words softly pencil onto the A4 page and save into your Study Again deck so you can jump straight back to any phrase and practice it.
  • Camera & Shortcuts: Move your cursor gently to look around the sunset cliff. P Play/Pause, Esc Song Menu, R Study Deck.
